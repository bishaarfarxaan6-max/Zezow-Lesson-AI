Code of Conduct

Our Commitment

Zezow Lesson AI is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone.

Our Standards

All community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and kind.
- Welcome beginners and experienced developers.
- Respect different opinions and backgrounds.
- Share knowledge and help others.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and offensive behavior.

Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, bullying, discrimination, insults, and disrespectful conduct are not accepted.

Our Responsibility

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these community standards.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Zezow Lesson AI community spaces.

Contact

For questions or concerns, please contact the Zezow Lesson AI project maintainers.

Thank you for helping build a positive learning community!
